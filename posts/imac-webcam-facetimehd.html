The iMac webcam works on Linux

by D'Vaughn House

The goal

Get the iMac's built-in FaceTime HD camera working under Arch Linux. A homelab server with a working camera is a nice bonus — video calls, quick captures, whatever.

Hardware and cost

  • 2017 27" iMac with FaceTime HD camera

Software stack

  • Arch Linux (Omarchy)
  • facetimehd-firmware — installed via yay (firmware.bin verified OK, boot image rebuilt)

What broke and how it was fixed

Short one: the camera needed its firmware, which Apple doesn't ship in a Linux-friendly way. Installing facetimehd-firmware via yay pulled it in, and after that /dev/video* showed up — video0, live — and the camera was working.

How the install command got into my terminal is the part I have to own: I pasted it by clipboard accident. It was sitting in my clipboard from somewhere, and there it was, in the terminal, waiting for Enter. Then I actually looked at what it was going to do, decided it was the right move, and ran it on purpose. Accidental paste, deliberate execution.

No driver compilation, no kernel patches, no drama. Sometimes the fix really is one package.

Verdict

If your Apple camera is dark under Linux, check for /dev/video* first — if it's missing, you almost certainly just need the firmware package. One install, done.
